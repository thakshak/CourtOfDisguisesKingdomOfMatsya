Lore & Win Conditions (5–10 Players)

Hidden heroes in King Virata's court must defend Matsya across 5 Battles against Trigarta and Hastinapura infiltrators.

  • Dharma Defenders Win: Succeed in 3 out of 5 Battles AND keep Kanka's true identity hidden at the end.
  • Infiltrators Win: Sabotage 3 out of 5 Battles, cause 5 consecutive council rejections (deadlocks), OR correctly unmask Kanka if Dharma wins 3 battles.

Secret Roles & Vision

  • Kanka (Dharma Advisor): Sees all Infiltrators except Kichaka. Must stay hidden from King Susharma.
  • Brihannala (Dharma Master): Sees Kanka and Chief Spy, but cannot tell which is which.
  • Ballava, Syrandhri, Granthika, Tantipala (Dharma Courtiers): No special vision; deduce allies through votes and battle outcomes.
  • King Susharma (Infiltrator Commander): Knows fellow Infiltrators (except Karna). Gets the final Unmasking guess if Dharma wins 3 battles.
  • Chief Spy (Infiltrator Envoy): Knows fellow Infiltrators (except Karna). Appears as Kanka to Brihannala.
  • Kichaka (Infiltrator General, 7+ players): Knows fellow Infiltrators (except Karna). Hidden from Kanka's vision.
  • Karna (Lone Infiltrator, 10 players): Does not know other Infiltrators, and they do not know Karna.

Turn Sequence & Battles

  1. Council Proposal: The Leader holding the Royal Signet selects the exact team size for the current battle and taps PROPOSE TEAM.
  2. Court Vote: All players vote Approve (YES) or Reject (NO). Majority YES sends the team to battle and resets deadlocks to 0. Tie or majority NO passes the Signet clockwise and adds 1 deadlock (5 in a row = Infiltrator win).
  3. Secret Battle: Chosen team members secretly play Defend or Sabotage (Dharma can only Defend). Any 1 Sabotage fails the battle — except Battle 4 in 7–10 player games, which requires 2 Sabotages to fail.
  4. Final Unmasking: If Dharma wins 3 battles, King Susharma gets one guess to unmask Kanka.
